# Faiza Ahmed Esha — Portfolio Adventure

This is a local, playable portfolio draft for review. It has not been published.

## Open the draft

Open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge. The portfolio works offline and needs no build tools or installation.

## Play

- Choose the girl or boy avatar; the pixel cat companion is optional.
- Walk along the curving waterfront street with the left and right arrows or A and D. Press Space or Up to jump.
- Press E or Enter at a place entrance to open that portfolio section.
- Press J or use Sections to open any part of the portfolio directly.
- Collect 14 coins for XP; each new section also adds XP.
- Switch between the neon night scene and the pastel day scene.
- On phones, use the on-screen walk, jump, and interact buttons.

The game is a scenic side-scrolling waterfront street with a gently curved road, city buildings, water, palms, lamps, birds, and eight separate places: About, Education, Experience, Projects, Achievements, Certificates, Skills, and Contact. Each place has a distinct entrance and a colorful animated portfolio pop-up. The Sections journal gives visitors a direct route to any stop.

Project cards link to the live sites and repositories listed in the CV. Certificate cards display the supplied certificate images, extracted from the attached PDFs, and open the full image when selected. The CV PDF is included for download.

The portfolio copy, contact details, links, dates, awards, project summaries, and certificate names are based on the supplied CV and certificate files. The logo, avatars, gray cat companion, pixel scenery, and section illustrations are original code-drawn artwork inspired by the visual references.
